Public memory benchmarks are easy to game: a lenient judge, an undisclosed
prompt, or a `top_k` larger than the corpus can move a score by tens of points.
The leaderboard exists to make every number checkable.

## Two tracks

| Track | What it measures | Judge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retrieval-only | Did the right evidence come back? | None; evidence IDs |
| End-to-end | Did the model answer correctly? | A disclosed LLM judge |

Track A is the honest floor. Track B is reported separately and never merged
into Track A.

## Rules

1. Report the mean of at least three runs, with standard deviation.
2. Disclose the judge model, the judge prompt, and a SHA-256 of that prompt.
3. Disclose ingestion: chunking, embedder, reranker, and contextual headers.
4. `top_k` must be smaller than the corpus. A submission with
   `top_k >= sessions_in_corpus` is rejected.
5. Publish per-question artifacts and the command that produced them.
6. Declare any post-processing. Unexplained deltas are rejected.
7. Retractions are permanent. The corrected row replaces the score, but the old
   row stays visible with a `retracted` note.

## Claims register

Numbers published without a runnable harness are listed for context, not
ranked:

| System | Claim | Track | Harness disclosed? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Living Brain | 92.2% judged accuracy, LoCoMo 1,540 q | end-to-end | No — "internal evaluations" |
| Mem0 | 68.5% LoCoMo (published) | end-to-end | Partial |
| Supermemory | 97% Recall@20, LongMemEval-S | retrieval | Aggregation method disclosed |

The LoCoMo answer key is roughly 6.4% wrong, so a perfect system caps out near
93.6%. A 92.2% claim sits close to that ceiling; it is not evidence of a
category lead.

## Submit

1. Add `benchmarks/leaderboard/<system>-<track>.json`.
2. Add per-question artifacts under `benchmarks/leaderboard/artifacts/<system>/`.
3. Open a PR titled `leaderboard: <system> <track> <dataset>`.
4. Include the reproduction command and the hardware used.

Corrections are welcome, including corrections to MemOS rows. The full spec
lives in
[`docs/benchmark-leaderboard.md`](https://github.com/Markgatcha/memos/blob/main/docs/benchmark-leaderboard.md).
